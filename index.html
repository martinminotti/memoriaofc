MEMORIA is the artistic convergence of three Italian DJs, producers and musicians — brought together by a deep-rooted friendship and years of creative synergy in the studio. Built on a shared musical vision, the project explores the relationship between sound, emotion and memory, translating personal experiences and shared moments into a distinctive musical language.

Musical Identity
04

Evocative.
Cinematic.

Rooted in Melodic and Progressive House, MEMORIA combines driving rhythms with poignant piano melodies, immersive synth textures and carefully crafted vocal elements. Select productions also incorporate subtle influences from Maccabi House, adding further character to their evolving sound.

Vocals play a central role in the trio’s sonic identity. Carefully selected and shaped through melodic phrasing and lyrical expression, they bring emotional depth to each composition, transforming individual tracks into immersive, memorable experiences.

Live Experience 08

Two ways
to listen.

MEMORIA presents its music through two distinct performance formats, each offering a different way to experience the project’s sonic identity.

Conceived as carefully structured sonic journeys rather than simple sequences of tracks. Guided by emotional progression and a strong sense of musical flow, their performances move from darker, introspective and underground territories towards expansive melodies and liberating synths.

Whether performing their own productions or music from other artists, MEMORIA builds a cohesive narrative designed to connect with the audience and evolve naturally throughout the set.

Blending a traditional DJ setup with live instrumentation, the hybrid format brings together CDJs, mixer, effects and live synthesizers or controllers.

Moving beyond conventional DJ performance, the trio integrates live interpretations of their own compositions with improvisation and real-time manipulation of sound — a dynamic performance that evolves with the moment, allowing each show to take on its own character.
